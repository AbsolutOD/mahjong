# TileTutor

TileTutor teaches beginners to read the American Mah Jongg card: what each printed line means as tiles, which lines a rack is closest to, and the rules that trip players up.

## Language

### The tiles

**Tile**:
One physical tile from the 152-tile American set.
_Avoid_: Piece

**Suit**:
One of the three numbered families: Dots, Bams or Craks.
_Avoid_: Colour (the card's colours are suit letters, not suits)

**Dragon**:
A tile tied to one suit: Red to Craks, Green to Bams, and the Soap to Dots.

**Soap**:
The white dragon, which also stands for zero in year lines such as `2026`.
_Avoid_: White dragon (when it is used as zero)

**Wind**:
One of North, East, West and South, which belong to no suit.

**Flower**:
A suitless tile. All flowers are interchangeable.

**Joker**:
A wildcard tile that may stand in only for a tile in a group of three or more identical tiles.

### The card

**Card**:
The full list of hands a player may win with, printed in shorthand.

**Practice card**:
TileTutor's own card of original hands in NMJL style. It contains none of NMJL's copyrighted text.
_Avoid_: NMJL card (for TileTutor's card)

**Section**:
A titled block of related hands on the card, such as Like Numbers or Quints.
_Avoid_: Category

**Hand**:
One winning pattern on the card, with its points and whether it is concealed.
_Avoid_: Pattern

**Line**:
The shorthand the card prints for one hand, such as `FF 2026 DDDD DDDD`.

**Group**:
A cluster of tiles inside a hand that the card prints as one unit.
_Avoid_: Meld, set

**Single / Pair / Pung / Kong / Quint / Sextet**:
A group of one, two, three, four, five or six *identical* tiles.

**Run**:
A group of tiles stepping up by one, such as `1234`. A run is never called a kong.

**Suit letter**:
A colour on the card, written A, B or C, that stands for a suit the player chooses. Different letters must be different suits.
_Avoid_: Suit variable, colour

**Binding**:
A choice of suit for each of a hand's suit letters.
_Avoid_: Assignment

**Concealed**:
A hand that must be completed with no exposed groups.

**Joker-eligible**:
A group that a joker may fill: three or more identical tiles. Singles, pairs, NEWS and year runs never are.

### The matcher and the Charleston

**Rack**:
The tiles a player holds, from none up to fourteen.
_Avoid_: Hand (for the tiles held)

**Tiles away**:
How many more tiles a rack needs to complete a hand at its best binding. Lower ranks higher.
_Avoid_: Score, distance

**Charleston**:
The opening exchange in which players pass three tiles at a time before play starts.

**Leading hands**:
The hands the Charleston advice is judged against: the three closest, or whichever hands the learner pins.

**Pin**:
A learner's choice to hold a hand among the leading hands whatever its rank.

**Wanted by**:
A rack tile is wanted by a hand when that hand at its best binding would use that copy.

**Spare**:
A rack tile that no leading hand wants, so it is safe to pass.

### Practice

**Round**:
A set of ten mixed questions ending on a score.
_Avoid_: Quiz, session

**Suit count**:
The number of different suit letters a line needs: one, two or three.

**Joker legality**:
Whether a joker may fill a given group.
